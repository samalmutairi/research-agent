# ZATCA e-invoicing: two implementation phases and Phase 2 start date

- **Date:** 2026-08-25
- **Question:** Per ZATCA's e-invoicing regulation, what are the two implementation phases and when did Phase 2 (integration) begin?
- **Route:** docs (zatca.gov.sa official)

## Answer

ZATCA's e-invoicing framework is implemented in two phases. Phase 1, the Generation Phase (مرحلة الإصدار والحفظ), required all persons subject to the E-Invoicing Regulation to generate and store electronic invoices and notes, effective 4 December 2021. Phase 2, the Integration Phase (مرحلة الربط والتكامل), requires integration of taxpayer e-invoicing solutions with ZATCA's FATOORA platform via API, and began 1 January 2023, rolled out in waves by targeted taxpayer group with at least six months' notice per wave. Both phases and dates are set in the resolution "Controls, Requirements, Technical Specifications and Procedural Rules for Implementing the Provisions of the E-Invoicing Regulation" (Governor's Decision No. 62738).
